Structure of a Mathematical Induction Proof.

To prove \(P(n), \forall n\geq 1\text{,}\)
  • Base Step.
    Show \(P(1)\) is true.
    🔗
  • Induction Step.
    Assume \(P(k)\) for some \(k\in\mathbb{Z}\text{,}\) show \(P(k+1)\text{.}\)
    🔗
Conclude \(P(n)\) is true for all \(n\geq 1\)
🔗
