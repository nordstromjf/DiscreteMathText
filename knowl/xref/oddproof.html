Activity 4.1.5.

In this activity practice using the proof writing advice. Think about what you need to assume and what you need to show. Make sure you do only one algebraic step at a time. Note, not all blocks may be used in the proof.
🔗
Prove if \(n\) is odd, then \(7n+2\) is odd.
🔗
Let \(n=2k+1\) for some \(k\in \mathbb{Z}\text{.}\)


---


\(7n+2=7(2k+1)+2\)

---


\(7n+2=7(2k)+7+2\)

---


\(7n+2=2(7k)+8+1\)

---


\(7n+2=2(7k+4)+1\)

---



\(7k+4\) is an integer.

---


Thus, \(7n+2\) is odd.

---


Let \(7n+2=2k+1\text{.}\)

 #distractor
---


Let \(k=7k+4\text{.}\)

 #distractor
---


\(7n+2=7(2k)+1+2\)
 #distractor
---


Let \(n=1\text{,}\) so \(7(1)+2=9\text{.}\)
