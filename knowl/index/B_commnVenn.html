Venn Diagrams for Common Statements.

  • All \(B\) are \(A\text{.}\)
    🔗
    
    One circle inside another circle. The outer circle is labeled “\(A\)”, the inner circle is labeled “\(B\)”.
    🔗
    🔗
  • Some \(A\) are \(B\text{,}\) or there exists an \(A\) that is a \(B\text{.}\)
    🔗
    
    Two circles next to each other with some overlapping area. The left circle is labeled “\(A\)”, the right circle is labeled “\(B\)”. There is an unlabeled point in the center of the overlapping area.
    🔗
    🔗
  • No \(A\) are \(B\text{.}\)
    🔗
    
    Two circles next to each other with no overlapping area. The left circle is labeled “\(A\)”, the right circle is labeled “\(B\)”.
